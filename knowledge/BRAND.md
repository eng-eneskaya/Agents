# Brand

## Voice & Tone
First-person and curious — the creator shows up as a genuine apprentice for a day, not a narrator lecturing from outside. Narration stays minimal; on-screen captions/text overlays carry facts (pay, tools, hours) when needed.

## Values
- Respect for the professionals and trades featured — never mocking or reducing the work to a gimmick
- Honesty about pay and difficulty — no exaggeration for clicks
- Immersive craftsmanship in the footage itself (ASMR-quality sound and visuals)

## Visual Style
- Ambient, high-fidelity sound design (tools, materials, environment) carries most scenes
- Clean, minimal on-screen captions/lower-thirds for facts (job title, pay, tools) instead of a talking-head narrator
- A consistent motion graphics template for recurring info overlays (e.g. a "salary reveal" graphic) across episodes, so the channel builds a recognizable visual identity

## Positioning
A global "try the job for a day" channel — starting with trades and everyday professions in Turkey (blacksmith, fisherman, cleaner) and expanding to skilled professions (doctor, developer) and international apprenticeships (e.g. a katana-making apprenticeship in Japan). Differentiated from typical "day in the life" vlogs by ASMR-grade production and hard facts (pay, tools) delivered through graphics rather than talking-head narration.
